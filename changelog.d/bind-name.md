## bind-name - `bindIn` is `bind`

The operator's word, minutes after it landed ("А можно bindIn назвать
просто bind?"). `p.bind(f: A => B ! G): B ! (F + G)` in RowLift; the
name was free (`Cont.bind(c)(f)` is a companion method, called
qualified, no extension in its way). `thenIn` keeps its name: `then`
is a keyword in Scala 3. `TestBindIn`, the guide and the spec section
say `bind`.
