## handle-on-machine — Effects.handle on the machine, measured and refuted

Operator ask, 2026-10-02 (sprint cont-js-depth step 2A). A translation of
`Effects.handle` onto the machine (the handler a `Delim` prompt, an
operation a `shift0`, `k` as data, the rest of the row forwarded) agreed
with the fold road on every answer and measured 1.74x slower and 1.74x
the bytes on one pre-built 10 000-operation program (306 vs 176 µs,
3.40 vs 1.96 MB; alternated, quiet box). The fold road stays — its
answer is a lazy program, so its strict `k` never nests. Probe deleted;
history.d handle-on-machine and specs/cont-js-depth.md 2A keep the record.
