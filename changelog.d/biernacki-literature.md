## biernacki-literature - Biernacki's publications mapped onto the backlog

Dariusz Biernacki's publication list (ii.uni.wroc.pl/~dabi/publications)
was read against okay, and each paper that applies was filed where its
work lives.

- Literature added to four existing items. `effect-instances-tunnelling`
  and `delim-region-prompts` get "Binders by day, labels by night"
  (POPL 2020), which gives one model for Tag, Refs and Delim.Stacked.
  `stacked-shift0` gets Materzok & Biernacki (ICFP 2011, APLAS 2012) and
  PPDP 2021 as the existing type system for `Below`.
  `continuations-as-data-spike` gets the functional-correspondence papers
  (PPDP 2003, LOPSTR 2021).
- Five new items: `handler-equivalence-oracle` (normal-form
  bisimilarity as an executable check for fusion lanes),
  `row-coercion-coherence-law` (widen vs normalize, TLCA 2015),
  `row-parametricity-forwarding-law` (Handle with care, POPL 2018),
  `monadic-reflection-stacked` (FSCD 2020) and, in dataflow,
  `clock-calculus-fusion` (LCTES 2008).
- The rest of the list is recorded as answered in
  `biernacki-literature-rest`.
- Found on the way: docs/many-instances.md and Instances.scala still
  said nothing checks `Tag` key distinctness. `Distinct[R]` has done that
  since 2026-09-11, and both now say so.
