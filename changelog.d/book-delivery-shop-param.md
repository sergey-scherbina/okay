## book-delivery-shop-param - chapter 16b: the shop is a parameter

The delivery `for` no longer chooses the shop: `shop` is a free variable
(`order(shop, item)`), so the same two lines — the price, then the fee —
are the whole program in all three versions. The expected answers are
per shop (north: two teas with delivery 50; south: one tea, free
delivery), asserted for cats, layered reflection and effects; the
refused cats `for` is re-pinned (the same two errors).
